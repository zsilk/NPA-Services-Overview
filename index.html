Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most from people deciding how to work with us.

Are you a fiduciary, and what does that mean for me?
Yes — and it’s something we’re proud of. When we provide advisory services, we act as a fiduciary, which means we’re obligated to act in your best interest. As part of an independent Registered Investment Adviser, our advice is built around your goals and your family. When insurance or brokerage products are involved, we’ll always explain how we’re compensated so you can decide with confidence.
How are your fees structured, and what will I actually pay?
Simple, transparent, and always in writing before you commit — no surprises. Our DIY model has no planning fee at all. Essential is a flat, one-time $1,500 fee for a complete, advisor-built plan that’s yours to keep. Premier is an all-in-one relationship billed as a percentage of the assets we manage that starts at 1.35% and decreases as your assets grow, covering both financial planning and active investment management. Your exact fee schedule is spelled out in your advisory agreement before you begin.
Do I need a minimum amount of money to get started?
There’s no minimum to start working with us — the best time is now, wherever you are. Anyone can begin with our DIY tools and a complimentary discovery meeting, and Essential planning is available no matter your account size. Ongoing Premier portfolio management is designed for households with $250,000 or more in investable assets. Not there yet? Start with an Essential plan — many clients grow into Premier over time. There’s a right place for everyone to begin.
What happens at the free discovery meeting?
It’s the easy first step. A relaxed, no-cost, no-obligation conversation with a real, local advisor — not a sales pitch. We’ll get to know you, answer your questions, and help you see exactly which service model fits your life. You’ll walk away with clarity and a clear next step, whether or not you decide to work with us.
Can I switch or upgrade service models later?
Of course — and many clients do. Each model is designed to build on the last, so you can start where you’re comfortable and grow with us over time. Even better: if you move up to Premier while we’re building your Essential plan, that one-time Essential fee is waived. You’re never tied to a long-term contract — just supported at every stage.
What if I already have an advisor? How do I switch?
Switching is usually easier than people expect — and we handle the heavy lifting. In many cases your accounts can transfer “in kind” (as they are), through a mostly paperless process, and we coordinate directly with the receiving custodian so you’re not stuck making awkward calls. First, we’ll give you an honest, no-pressure review of what you have today — and if staying where you are truly makes more sense for you, we’ll tell you. There’s no cost to explore it, and you’re never obligated to move. Please note your current firm may charge account transfer or termination fees, certain holdings may not transfer in kind, and a transfer could have tax consequences — so we’d encourage you to consult your tax professional before making changes.
Why work with you instead of a DIY app or robo-advisor?
DIY tools and robo-advisors can be a great starting point — in fact, we offer self-service tools too. But an algorithm can’t sit across from you when markets get bumpy, doesn’t know your family, and won’t proactively reach out about a tax opportunity or a major life change. Premier gives you a real, local advisor who builds a strategy around your goals and actively manages it — plus proactive, tax-aware planning, estate and legacy coordination, and priority access when life happens. Technology is a helpful tool; personal judgment and an ongoing relationship are the difference. Keep in mind all investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal, and no strategy or level of service can guarantee a profit or protect against loss.
What do terms like “fiduciary,” “rebalancing,” and “in-kind transfer” actually mean?
Plain English, no jargon. A fiduciary is required to act in your best interest — not just recommend what’s “suitable.” Rebalancing means periodically adjusting your investments back to your target mix so your risk level doesn’t drift over time. An “in-kind” transfer moves your existing investments between firms as they are, without selling them first. A Registered Investment Adviser (RIA) is a firm registered to give investment advice and held to that fiduciary standard. And assets under management is simply the total value of the accounts we manage for you — the basis for Premier’s fee. Curious about anything else? Ask at your discovery meeting — we’re happy to translate.
